02 • THE PROBLEM

Managing Money Is Hard

Untracked Spending

People often lose track of where their money goes.

Poor Budgeting

Without a clear budget, it is difficult to control daily expenses.

Limited Awareness

Many people discover they have overspent only when their money runs out.

03 • THE SOLUTION

Meet SpendWise

SpendWise makes personal money management easier by helping users:

  • Record and categorize daily expenses.
  • Create budgets for different spending categories.
  • Monitor spending against their budgets.
  • Understand their financial habits.

Our goal: Make budgeting simple, clear, and accessible.

05 • MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Who Needs SpendWise?

Our initial target customers are people who want more control over their personal finances.

  • University and college students.
  • Young professionals.
  • People managing a limited monthly income.
  • Individuals who want to build better saving habits.

We will begin by validating demand among users in Kenya before expanding to other markets.
